# I: Purposes and Definitions of Rhetoric

Book I lays the groundwork for the rest of the work: what rhetoric is for,
how it relates to dialectic, and the three species an orator works in
(deliberative, forensic, and epideictic). It then works through the
subject-matter each species draws on — the good, happiness, virtue, the forms
of government, and the workings of praise, accusation, and legal judgment —
before closing with the mechanics of a trial.
