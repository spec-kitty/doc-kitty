# About and license

The [Rhetoric section](/rhetoric/) is a real, worked example: a conversion of
Aristotle's *Rhetoric* into doc-kitty pages, used to show a full Markua-authored
work rendering natively. It is **not** a copy of the book itself — it is a
docsite conversion of one existing translation, restructured, lightly edited
for the modern reader, and annotated with editor's notes.

## The Perseus Project

The [Perseus Project](http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper) — also known as the
Perseus Hopper — is a long-running online digital library curated by the
Department of Classical Studies at Tufts University. As an open source
project, it has had many contributors since its founding in 1987. It is named
after the Greek hero Perseus, the mythical slayer of monsters and legendary
founder of Mycenae.

Tufts University aims to provide a comprehensive catalogue of texts from the
ancient world so modern audiences can access them freely, and licenses its own
work accordingly to enable that free sharing of knowledge.
