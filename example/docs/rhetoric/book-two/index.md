# II: Persuasion techniques

Book II turns from Book I's definitions and contexts to the technical core of
persuasion: the credibility of the speaker (`ethos`), the emotional state of
the audience (`pathos`), and the patterns of reasoning an orator relies on
(`logos`). Aristotle works through each mode in turn, including a systematic
survey of the emotions and a catalogue of common argument patterns and their
pitfalls.

This showcase converts [Book I](/rhetoric/book-one/) only — Book II's
chapters are out of scope here. This page is a landing only, so the section
stays navigable without dangling links.
