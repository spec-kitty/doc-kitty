# Glossary

The vocabulary of each bounded context. Terms are grouped by the context that owns them.

- [hr](./hr/) — How the organisation hires, supports, and develops the people who work in it.
- [rhetoric](./rhetoric/) — The art of finding the available means of persuasion.
- [shipping](./shipping/) — How physical goods move from a seller to a buyer across carriers and borders.
