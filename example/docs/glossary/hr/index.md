# hr

How the organisation hires, supports, and develops the people who work in it.

## policy {#policy}

A written rule that governs how employees are expected to act — a leave
rule or a code of conduct, for instance — approved by the people team.

**Examples:**

- A remote-work rule setting core collaboration hours.

## onboarding {#onboarding}

The structured first weeks of a new hire's employment — access, training,
and introductions — until they can work without close guidance.

**Examples:**

- A thirty-day plan with a named buddy and a week-one checklist.
