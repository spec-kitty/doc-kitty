# Why a cap

Starlight lists every page of a folder in the sidebar of every page. For a
folder with a few pages that is what a reader wants. For a folder with hundreds
of generated pages, every page of the site carries hundreds of links, and the
build renders them again for every page.

A hub over its cap keeps its overview link and one entry that leads to the hub,
where the pages are listed.
